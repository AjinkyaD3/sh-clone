Have you ever wondered how long it would take for a burglar to break through your front door? Your security starts at the entrance. Our high-security doors are rigorously tested to determine how long they can withstand break-in attempts using different tools—from basic hand tools to powerful electric equipment. Security levels, like EN 1627), indicate the time and effort needed to force entry. The higher the rating, the tougher the door.

Don’t leave your safety to chance—choose a door that keeps intruders out and peace of mind in!
