A bespoke security door frame

With a door frame of up to 115 mm of thick steel, our solutions offer bespoke security for homes, garage doors and businesses. Designed to withstand the impact of heavy duty tools such as crowbars, hammers and even drills, you can rest assured that our front doors will keep away any wrong-doer.

Multipoint locking system

Our doors feature a 12-point locking system with 10 points on the side and 2 additional ones going in an up and down direction. Additionally, the locks themselves are designed with special steel enhancements protecting both its body and rods. Combine that with additional manganese plate against drilling and you got yourself a truly sturdy security lock.

Specially designed door hinges

Door hinges are an integral part of the overall sturdiness of the door construction. The ones we offer are specially reinforced and can be easily adapted to create any bespoke door solution desired.

Unmatched insulation characteristics

Our security doors also feature a multilayer solution in terms of insulation. Layers of up to 60mm in rockwool provide excellent sound and thermal insulation, meaning you don’t need to sacrifice average comforts for the sake of security.

Flawless design finishes

A capable high security door solution with a strong steel frame does not mean that you have to compromise the looks of your home. Regardless of what style you are going for, our variants allow you to personalize your high security doors in accordance with exact specifications you provide.

Longevity & durability

Our security doors are made to last. This means that not only will you have a means for high security front doors right now, but for years to come. We only use the high quality materials that ensure a door has plenty of longevity and maintains its security features.

Of course, regular maintenance is still recommended as a preventative measure to identify and address any issues to ensure that your doors last for years to come. With proper installation and regular upkeep, the product will last for several decades.

How to choose security doors?

Choosing a security door option for your home might not be as easy as one might think. After all, there are many factors to keep in mind such as the features of the steel security door itself, the door furniture and panels, hinges, multi point locking systems and so on, not to mention the finished design of your doors as well.

Depending on what’s needed, careful planning and eventual construction has to be undertaken to ensure that no place at your front door is left vulnerable for potential threats.

When considering what type security doors to choose from a security firm, we recommend evaluating:

  • Official safety grade of the security doors offered
  • Door locking system implemented
  • Available door furniture personalization options
  • Door design customizations offered

What are security doors, and why do I need one for my home or business?

Security doors are specialized doors designed to enhance the safety and protection of your property. They are constructed with durable materials and advanced locking mechanisms to deter burglars and intruders. Installing security doors is crucial for safeguarding your family, belongings, and property, reducing the risk of break-ins.

Are security doors only suitable for residential properties, or can they be used in commercial settings as well?

Security doors are versatile and can be installed in both residential and commercial properties. They are a valuable addition to businesses, especially those with valuable assets, as they provide an extra layer of protection during non-operational hours.

How do security doors differ from standard doors in terms of construction and materials?

Security doors are built with sturdier materials, such as steel frames and solid cores, making them more resistant to forced entry. They often feature multi-point locking systems and reinforced hinges. In contrast, standard doors are typically made for aesthetics and may not provide the same level of security.

Can I customize the design of my front doors to match my property’s aesthetics?

Yes, most security door providers offer customization options. You can choose from various styles, colours, and finishes to ensure that your security door complements your property’s architecture and enhances its curb appeal.

Are security doors weather-resistant and suitable for all climates?

Yes, a quality, secure door, unlike a normal door, is designed to withstand various weather conditions. They are often equipped with insulation that provides thermal and weatherproofing properties, ensuring your home remains comfortable and secure year-round.

Do security doors also offer soundproofing benefits?

Many security doors, for example, a set of steel doors, come with soundproofing features due to their robust construction and insulation layers. While they may not eliminate all outside noise, they can significantly reduce it, providing a quieter and more peaceful indoor environment.

Are security doors difficult to install, and can they replace my existing doors?

Security doors are professionally installed by experts to ensure they fit perfectly and provide maximum security. They can be installed as primary entry front doors or used in conjunction with other doors to enhance security further.

Do security doors come with warranty coverage?

Most reputable security door providers offer warranties on their products. The length and terms of the warranty may vary, so it’s essential to inquire about the specific warranty details when making a purchase.

Can security doors be fire-resistant as well?

Some security steel door options come with fire-resistant features and can be customized to meet specific fire safety standards. Be sure to discuss your fire safety requirements with your security door provider.

How can I choose the right security door for my property?

Selecting the right security steel door involves considering factors such as your property’s security needs, design preferences, and budget. Consult with a security door specialist who can assess your requirements and provide tailored recommendations to meet your needs effectively.

Remember that investing in a high-quality security door is a proactive step towards safeguarding your property and ensuring peace of mind for you and your family or business.
