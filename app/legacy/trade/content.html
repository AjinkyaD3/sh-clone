Custom-made security door frame

Our high security doors are equipped with a bespoke door frame made of robust steel, measuring up to 115 mm in thickness. This custom design ensures unparalleled security for residential, garage, and commercial applications. Engineered to withstand heavy-duty tools like crowbars, hammers, and drills, our front doors provide a formidable barrier against intruders

Advanced multipoint locking system

Our doors feature an advanced 12-point locking system, with 10 points securing the sides and 2 additional points vertically. The locks themselves are reinforced with special steel enhancements, safeguarding both the lock body and rods. Additionally, a manganese plate adds protection against drilling, making our security lock exceptionally sturdy.

Reinforced door hinges

The strength of door hinges is crucial to the overall durability of the door. Our specially reinforced hinges can be easily adapted to create customized door solutions, ensuring the utmost sturdiness.

Exceptional insulation

Our security doors incorporate a multilayer insulation solution, with layers of up to 60mm of rockwool. This not only provides outstanding sound insulation but also offers thermal insulation, allowing you to enjoy comfort without compromising security.

Impeccable design finishes

A high security door with a robust steel frame doesn’t mean sacrificing aesthetics. Regardless of your preferred style, our door variants enable you to personalize your high security doors to precise specifications, ensuring a seamless blend with your property’s design.

Longevity and durability

Our security doors are built to withstand the test of time. We use only high-quality materials that guarantee long-lasting durability while maintaining top-notch security features.

Selecting the perfect door for your needs

Choosing the right security doors, requires careful consideration of several factors. It’s not just about the door itself; it’s a comprehensive process involving various elements such as door features, furniture, panels, hinges, multi-point locking systems, and the door’s final design.

Read more

Whether you need high-security solutions for existing doors or plan to install a new set of doors for your home, business, or garage properties, rest assured that we are equipped to address all your security requirements.

When contemplating security door options from wholesale manufacturers and suppliers, we recommend evaluating the following:

1. Official safety grade: Assess the safety grade of the security doors offered, ensuring they meet your security standards.
2. Locking system: Examine the door locking system in place, as it is a critical component of security.
3. Customization: Explore door furniture personalization options to tailor the doors to your specific needs.
4. Design customization: Consider design customizations available to ensure the doors complement your property’s aesthetics.
5. Additional security features: Inquire about supplementary security enhancements for your front doors, such as bulletproofing and drill-proofing, to fortify your property against potential threats.
