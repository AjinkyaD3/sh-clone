Frequently asked uestions (FAQ)

What are bulletproof doors, and how do they work?

Making sure that a household, communal or business area has the highest level of security should be a top priority for anyone who wants safety for their goods and well-being.

A bespoke front door security option in that overall setup is also important, even though sometimes both home and business owners don’t seem to realise that. In fact, statistics indicate that almost 70 percent of all break-ins nowadays happen through the front doors.

Often referred to as the most vulnerable part in the overall security of a residential or business area, front entrance doors require extreme attention to detail and a well-thought-out design, in order to offer the maximum protection possible, especially in hostile environments.

Bulletproof doors are designed to resist gunfire and provide protection in high-risk environments. They consist of layers of materials, such as steel and ballistic glass, that absorb and disperse the energy from bullets, preventing them from penetrating the door.

What types of buildings or spaces commonly use bulletproof doors?

Bulletproof doors are often found in places like banks, government buildings, jewellery stores, police stations, and safe rooms in residential properties. They are used in locations where security and protection from potential threats are paramount.

Are bulletproof doors completely impenetrable?

While bulletproof doors are highly resistant to gunfire, they are not entirely impenetrable. The level of protection depends on the door’s rating, which is determined by the materials and design. Higher-rated doors offer greater resistance to bullets and other threats.

How do I choose the right bulletproof door for my needs?

Choosing the right bulletproof door involves considering factors such as the desired level of protection (ballistic rating), the type of building or space, budget, and aesthetic preferences. Consulting with a security expert can help you make an informed decision.

Are bulletproof doors aesthetically pleasing, or do they look bulky and unattractive?

Modern ballistic steel doors are designed with aesthetics in mind. Many options are available, including doors that resemble standard doors, allowing you to maintain the desired look while ensuring security.

Can I retrofit an existing door with bulletproof features?

Yes, it is possible to retrofit an existing door with bulletproof features. However, it is often more effective and aesthetically pleasing to install a purpose-built bulletproof door that meets your specific security requirements.

Do bulletproof doors provide protection against other threats, such as fire or forced entry?

Some bulletproof doors may also offer fire resistance, and many are also designed to resist forced entry. The level of protection against these threats may vary, so it’s essential to inquire about specific features when selecting a door.

Are bulletproof doors expensive?

The cost of bulletproof doors varies significantly depending on factors such as size, ballistic rating, materials, and additional features. While they can be more expensive than standard doors, the level of protection they provide justifies the investment in high-risk environments.

How often should bulletproof doors be inspected and maintained?

Bulletproof doors should be inspected regularly to ensure they remain in good condition. Maintenance intervals can vary but typically include checks for any damage or wear to the door, frames, hinges, and locks. Consult the manufacturer’s guidelines for specific recommendations.

Can bulletproof doors be customised to fit unique architectural requirements?

Yes, many manufacturers offer customisation options to ensure that bulletproof doors can seamlessly integrate into your building’s architectural design. A custom bulletproof door set can come in bespoke sizes, styles, and finishes.
