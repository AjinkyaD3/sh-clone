Frequently asked questions (FAQ)

What are fire resistant doors?

Fire resistant doors from Secure House are specially designed to withstand high temperatures and prevent the rapid spread of fire and smoke, providing critical protection for both people and property during a fire.

How do fire resistant doors work and why are they important?

Secure House explains that fire resistant doors are made from fire‑rated materials and tested to strict safety standards, helping contain fire and smoke long enough for occupants to evacuate safely.

What fire resistance ratings are available for fire resistant doors?

At Secure House, fire resistant doors come with various ratings such as FD30 and FD60, which indicate how many minutes the door can resist fire and heat while maintaining structural integrity.

Can fire resistant doors also provide protection against smoke?

Yes, Secure House fire resistant doors are designed to restrict the movement of smoke in addition to flames, which is often critical in ensuring safe evacuation during a fire.

Do fire resistant doors require special glass if they have viewing panels?

If a fire resistant door includes a viewing panel, Secure House uses certified fire‑resistant glass to maintain the door’s safety rating and prevent smoke or flames from passing through.

How do fire resistant doors differ from regular doors?

Fire resistant doors from Secure House are built with fire‑rated materials, intumescent seals, and tested hardware, unlike regular doors, which do not offer any significant protection against fire or smoke.

Can fire resistant doors be used in both homes and commercial buildings?

Secure House supplies fire resistant doors suitable for both residential properties and commercial buildings, ensuring safety across stairwells, corridors, apartments, and workplaces.

How long should fire resistant doors last in an emergency?

The fire rating of Secure House fire resistant doors, ranging from 30 to 120 minutes, indicates how long the door can contain fire and smoke, providing occupants with valuable time to evacuate safely.
